# Requested DfAM assessment

Use this protocol only for a requested broad DfAM assessment of a Fusion design or specified parts; routine modeling, a single fit, or a targeted orientation or overhang check does not need it. Get printing rules and methods from relevant [fusion-print](.agents/skills/fusion-print/SKILL.md) references. Keep verified project facts and accepted decisions in `Project.md`.

Establish printed components, process, material, nozzle, layer height, and orientation from the request and verified context. State missing inputs and assumptions; do not present defaults as decisions. Include nonprinted and purchased parts only where they constrain fit, assembly, or printing.

Assess the printed parts together: orientation and bed contact; supports, bridges, overhangs; minimum features and fits; oriented strength and stiffness; assembly access and postprocessing. Use measurements or observed geometry to distinguish problems from near-limit rules. Trace actionable findings to controlling sketches or features where possible.

Report actionable findings in a short priority table: part/feature, evidence and print consequence, proposed change, effects on function or other parts, and disposition (safe within edit scope, acceptable as-is, or needs a user decision). Identify checks needing slicer, material, or print evidence. Assessment alone does not authorize model edits. For requested improvement, use the skill's improvement reference, verify the design, and record confirmed outcomes and accepted decisions in `Project.md`.
